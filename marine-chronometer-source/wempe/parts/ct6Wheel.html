Notes. Every size is an estimate from photographs of Kirov No. 247 and Romanov's figures; no works drawing of the 6MX's contact exists. The numbers are the page's (dims.js CONTACT6, CT6). It is riveted on its collet, on the seconds arbor under the seconds wheel. The page's teeth are chords of the spiral (6 to a slope) and of the root circle; the recipe draws the spiral as a spline and the root as arcs.

Threads drawn by convention (DIN ISO 6410): the STEP solid has an outside thread at its major diameter, an inside one at its minor (tap drill).
