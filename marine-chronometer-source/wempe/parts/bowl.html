Notes. The sheet is a casting's: nothing machined is drawn but the recess for the closing plate and the holes; the rim's thread, rebate, notch and the faced floor are the model's (case.js, from 05 01 01 and the 6MX's bowls). The case brackets' screws (WN-78) are tapped into the wall at assembly, where the brackets are moved to level the bowl ("Kloben verstellen"); the page draws no hole for them. The sheet gives the weight as 350 g, light for brass.

Threads drawn by convention (DIN ISO 6410): the STEP solid has an outside thread at its major diameter, an inside one at its minor (tap drill).
