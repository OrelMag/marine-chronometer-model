The Soviet 6MX's block: 05 04 01-4's form (its base, thin part, upright), read in first angle; the upright's end slit along the plate as the German blocks' (Kirov 247, 24020); what clamps it to the plate is not seen (README, Unresolved).

Where the page's model departs from this recipe (the drawing's figure is this sheet's): the banking screw's hole across the leaves: the page draws it plain, a bore r 0.62 round the shank's 0.6, the recipe tapped M1.2 at the tap drill (the threads' convention; a hole across has no thread zone) the end face's M1: the page draws the 6MX's hole at the thread's major diameter (r 0.5, a polygon), where the 1943 block's is at the minor (0.729): the recipe taps it M1 at the tap drill the stop screw's hole: the page draws a polygon bore of r 0.70 (the thread's major), the recipe taps it M1.4 at the tap drill (a hole across has no thread zone)

Threads drawn by convention (DIN ISO 6410): the STEP solid has an outside thread at its major diameter, an inside one at its minor (tap drill).
