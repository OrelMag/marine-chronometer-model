## Method (and the weak links)

Everything absolute rests on link 1.

1. **Link 1 (sure, +-0.4 mm).** `1_Kirow_247_Z.jpg` (dial face-on, bezel off): ellipses fitted to the gilt flange's outer edge and to the bowl's outermost rim edge (`rim_ellipses.py`: per-ray edge candidates, trimmed fit, rms 0.2 px, both axes agree to 0.2 %). Flange 937.6 px = 105.95 mm gives 8.85 px/mm (cross-check: sub-dial centres 405 px apart for 46 mm = 8.80 px/mm). Rim outer / flange outer = 1.039 (axes 1.0386 and 1.0397). So the **outermost edge face-on = 110.1 mm +-0.4**, at the flange's depth (the flange is flush with the rim). That edge is the threaded band (ridges run to it; no separate wider ring is visible). The collar is wider (below), but sits about 12 mm deeper, so a close camera shrinks it to the same size face-on: it cannot be read from this photo.
2. **Link 2 (likely).** Ratios inside one side photo. `13_Kirow_2973_E-Kontakt4.jpg`, silhouette widths: thread 972 px, collar 1003 px, body just under the collar 938-944 px, so collar/thread 1.032 and collar/body 1.064. DEC 422 (bowl turned over, near level view) gives collar/body 1.057-1.065 independently. With thread = 110.1 mm: collar 113.6 +-1, body 106.8-107.4. Camera elevation (magnitude) from the back/front arc of the rim (`elev_from_arc.py`, poor fits, rms 9-21 px, read +-8 deg): 2973 about 19 deg, 247 about 26 deg, 24020 about 39 deg. The 247 and 2973 side views look from below the rim plane (the rim's top edge is a frown, and no inside is visible); 24020 looks from above. Axial lengths therefore carry cos(el) = 0.95 / 0.90 / 0.78.
3. DEC 422 (`11_Kirow_DEC_422_Gehäuse.jpg`, copied to dec422_case.jpg) is the only near-level side view of a whole bowl with the bezel on. **Its scale is not independent**: 8.2 px/mm was chosen so that its collar equals 113.6 mm. The right silhouette edge was read by gradient (`profile_right.py`, axis x = 505 px +-5, piecewise-line fit); the left edge is unreliable (background shading), so radii use the right edge only. DEC 422 may be a German-made transitional piece (ranked lowest), but its proportions agree with 2973 (body 36.7 against 36 mm; collar/body 1.06 against 1.064).
4. Pitch and ridge counts: by eye on 4x-8x crops plus row-mean peaks (`ridge_rows.py`). The silhouette shows no resolvable thread ripple (`thread_profile.py`): too fine and blurred.

Not done: an mfgfit `profile_` fit (no trace and no camera for these photos; I used silhouette gradients and ellipse fits, a lesser tool). The thread's hand could not be read.

## Surprises and flags

* The collar is clearly wider than the thread (3 %, about 3.5 mm in Ø) and a plain neck of about 2.8 mm separates them; the thread does not run into the collar.
* The bezel is not a plain knurled cylinder: on DEC 422 a smooth bell flares from the bowl side, then a plain band, and the diamond knurl is a band of about 5.6 mm at the glass end. 09777 looks the same.
* The bezel stops about 1.5 mm short of the collar, so a strip of thread shows.
* Thread pitch is known only to about 1.0 +-0.25 mm; its hand is not known.
* One notch in the rim, 1.5 mm wide, only seen on 24020.
* The foot has a lip ring, a flat disc, a central slotted plug and one off-centre hole (14100, moMvyx, 422).
* The 247 outline is not resolvable at the foot (no photo of its foot); every foot reading is from 14100, 422 and moMvyx.
