The scans are 1024 px on the long side, so at 5:1 one model millimetre is about 12-18 px and at 2:1 about 5-13 px: every number below was read on a crop enlarged 3-12 times (crops in the session scratchpad, `dc2/b7/`, named per sheet: `s01a`, `g01a` etc.; `g*` crops carry a grid in original pixels). Where a figure is not legible at that size it says so. Pixel measurements are given only to say which edges a dimension joins; they are never a substitute for a figure.
