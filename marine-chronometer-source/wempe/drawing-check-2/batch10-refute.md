# Batch 10, the refuter: the case, gimbals, closing plate, bezel and glass, the two keys, the boxes, handle, maker's plate and tongue

Second drawing check (PLAN-wempe-drawing-check-2.md, step 7), 28 October 2026. Worked from the sheets (05-01_1 to _9, 51-00_1 to _5, 05-02_01), Wempe 3955's photograph `16_Wempe_3955_Gehäusetopf_Kardan.jpg` and the built model (worktree `wempe-check2-audit3`, main d178fd63, read only), without the reader's or the comparer's reasoning until the verdicts below were written.

Method. Crops with PIL (`python -P`), scale from each sheet's own figures. Edge positions as darkness-weighted centroids along rays or columns, circles by least squares. The model probed with a Playwright script on `wempe/index.html?snap&qa` (MC_GL=gpu), through `__BX.root` and `__mv.userData.setRev`, in last, first, 1943, 3955 and 6mx: the closing plate's world vertices (the angles of its holes, the heights of its bore and counterbore), WN-71's radii and heights, the 1940 key's pieces, the maker's plate's geometries, the outer box's strip. Scripts and crops in the scratchpad `dc2\b10r\` (`probe.py`, `probe2.py`, `fitr4.py`, `tg.py`, `tgl.py`, `crop.py`).

## After reading batch10-model.md

The comparer and I agree on C1 (the same atan2 figures, 197.5° and 107.5°), C2, C3, C5, C6, C8, C9, C10 and C11, and on the bezel's glass. Where we part:

- **C4 (their M4: "r 6.3, wrong, small").** They fit the line's centres and get 6.3 with a 0.6 px residual. My fit tracks the line along rays and refits the centre, and gives 6.95 with a 0.07 px residual over 95-173°. Its centre is tangent to both the wall's and the floor's lines (23.6 and 24.2 px against r 24.1). The same method gives 6.2 over the middle 30° only. Their 6.3 is the curvature of the drawn round's middle. A fillet has to meet both lines, and the circle that does is r 7. They also note, rightly, that the scan draws the bowl's foot and cone out of scale. Taken together, an unfigured round can't be called 0.7 wrong from this scan. I'd drop M4, or carry it as "6.2-7.0 drawn; 7 kept" in the README's Estimated section.
- **C7, the screw's size.** They read the head as Ø 2.9 and the shank as Ø 1.8 × 6.8. On the scan the sink's edges at the cap's face are 27.5 px apart (Ø 4.0) and the shank's lines 15.5 px apart (Ø 2.3), at the sheet's 6.79 px/mm. Their 5.7 off the axis agrees with mine. They also don't note a second screw: the section's outside half shows another countersunk head at −5.85, which the front view draws on the axis with the first. If M7 is built, it should take two screws, about M2 with Ø 4 heads.
- **C15's attribution.** They give the title block "So 127 556 04" as the lever chronometer's Werk number. The same number heads the Einheits' own fusee and barrel sheets (05 06_1, 05 07_1, 05 05_1), so it can't decide the question by itself. Their conclusion holds on other grounds: 05 02_1's hand-setting pinion and its going "Federhaus", and 51-00_1's dial with "Ruhe / Lauf" and no up-and-down. The ledger should give those grounds rather than the title block.
- **The tongue (their "match for every figure").** They didn't check the round end. Its "6" is a radius: R 6 about the Ø 6.2 hole, where the model has a semicircle r 5 centred 1 beyond it. This should join M9. Their M9 row also gives the model's taper as "3.2° (2 over 36)". As built it is 1.83 over 33 to the step, then on to 8 at the end. Either way it is neither the sheet's 2°40' nor 1°20' an edge.
