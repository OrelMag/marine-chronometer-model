# Batch 1, blind reading: balance, hairspring, stud, collet, rollers, staffs

Second drawing check (PLAN-wempe-drawing-check-2.md), steps 1-3, by the blind reader, 27 October 2026.

Steps 1-2 for every sheet were written from the sheet images alone, before any transcription, code or earlier report was opened. Step 3 follows them.

The sheets are scans of 1024 px on their long side. At that resolution a 5:1 sheet gives about 17 px per mm and a 2:1 sheet about 7 px per mm, so a dimension's figures are legible and its arrowheads can be placed to about a pixel. Crops were enlarged 2-16 times with autocontrast. They are in the scratch folder `C:\Users\USER\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\e--Marine-Chronometer\68700a8e-be00-4ada-9796-297f431020bf\scratchpad\dc2\b1\` and are cited below by file name (`s9_sq2.png` and so on). Where a crop is cited as evidence, the measured span is given in original-image pixels.

Conventions: "Ø" is a diameter, "r" a radius, "( )" a reference (bracketed) dimension, and "[ ]" a boxed dimension ("eingerahmte Maße werden bei der Abnahme besonders geprüft", checked specially at acceptance).
