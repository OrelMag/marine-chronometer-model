## After reading batch11-model.md: the disagreements

1. **J42, the hands ("holds").** Its arrangement column has the section's blades (13.2..14.0, 15.6..16.1), but its model column is the hands' whole ranges with their bosses (12.1..15.2, 15.7..17.7). Blade against blade the model is 1.2 (hour) and 0.7-0.8 (minute) farther from the dial, and the hands aren't in its M3 list. dial.js says the model does this on purpose: the hour wheel's pipe is 8.2 overall (05 02 03), and the tube's square leaves the minute hand no room lower. So the sheets may well decide, and I don't ask for a model change. But M3 should list the hands with their measured 1.2 and 0.75, and dial.js's "about 2 lower" (lines 100 and 135) should say 1.2 and 0.75. Likely.
2. **J29-J31, the maintaining stack ("holds (the overlay)", "to 1-2 px").** I read it 0.45-0.7 higher on the section, from dark ink lines at rows 487 and 511 (x 935-950), against the model's rays at r 9-17 about the fusee. These are sharp lines, not wash, so I don't think the stack lines up to 1-2 px. The fusee arbor's and wheels' own sheets may place it (batch 5), in which case it belongs in M3. Either way it can't stand as "lines up". Likely.
3. **J21, the cork ring ("holds", rows 140-172).** Its top line is at row 135 in every column (grey 115-147 at x 725, 735, 745, 860), not 140: −43.21 against the model's −42.65, 0.56. The cork has no sheet and its sizes are "scaled on the section", so the section should decide it. A small correction (raise its top 0.5, or say why not). Likely.
4. **The lower escape stones (in the overlay list as "the escape arbor's jewels … to 1-2 px").** The section's lower hole jewel is 0.8 thick (0.6..1.4), the model's 0.44 (1.34..1.78, escapement.js's `lT = 0.45`, the balance's form scaled, "estimated"). The endstone is 0.75 against 0.42. The pivot's tip and the endstone's face agree; the stones' thickness doesn't. Since the model calls them estimated, the section is the evidence. Likely.
5. **F3/M1, WN-18.** The comparer reads a closed lower loop with a waist at 24×. Beside the section's own "8" in "05 08 00-3" (`cmp08.png`) the glyph is fainter and more open than that 8. I can't tell 0 from 8 there. The argument that one WN number can't name M1 and M1.2 uses threads the model estimated, since no WN sheet is in the set. I would not rename on this. At most, record both readings in the README's Readings corrected and keep WN-10 until another source decides. Possible.
6. **J59/M7, the banking screw's name.** The 1941 screw's line `cone41` already has `dwg: "WN-16"`, the name "WN-16 by the coloured section's label" and the card's "WN-16". M7's correction ("name the banking screw WN-16 on its bom line and card") is done for cone41. It applies to WN-gkA43 / WN-gkA only if the section draws the 1943 screw, and README 876 says it draws the 1941 one. Possible.
7. **05-38 ("none of 05-38's parts or places is the 6MX's").** The parts, yes, and the screws' places (56/57 against the 6MX base screws, as the comparer measured too). But its contact wheel is likely on the seconds arbor beside the lower cock: the arbor runs out through the Unterplatte, and the cock is filed to clear the wheel. That is where the 6MX's contact wheel is. Likely.
8. **Agreements worth adding to M5.** "Unruhkloben unten 04" and "…mitte 04" on 05-38 are 127-556.04 parts. 127-556.04 is this calibre's number: the dial 05 02 00-4 is 127-556.04-162, under the same "LC" stamp that 05-36 and 05-36_2 carry. So the README's "127-556 is the Luftwaffe's lever chronometer BC 1010" is wrong as a reason to set 05-33, 05-36 and 05-38 aside. The "LC" stamp doesn't mark a lever chronometer's sheet. This supports the comparer's F9/M5.
9. **Small readings, no disagreement in kind.** The stud's bar top is −39.3, not −38.9 (row 171, not 175), 0.45 over the model's: the stud's own sheet decides (batch 1). The dial pin runs from 5.85 to 11.1 through the dial on the section's left end, not 5.7 to 10.6. The left end's dial is 0.4 off its right end, as the comparer's overlay note 5 says, so no finding. The up-and-down stud and pipe reach 1.0 farther below the dial's face in the model (12.30, 12.35) than on the section (flush, 11.2-11.3). If their sheets (05 02 00-6, 05 02 04-2) give the model's lengths, it belongs in M3.

Everything else in batch11-model.md that I checked stands: J1-J20, J23, J26-J28, J32, J34-J41, J43-J58, J60-J70, F1, F7 (the WN-12a reading), F10's hole places, M6, M9, M11 and the 001 mappings.
