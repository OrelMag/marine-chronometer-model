# Drawing check 2, batch 7: the refuter's report

The motion work, the up-and-down work, the hands and the dial. Sheets: `References/wempe/drawings/05-02/Wempe_05-02_01.jpg` to `_13.jpg` and `05-05/Wempe_05-05_08.jpg`.
The model is the read-only worktree `wempe-check2-audit2`, detached at efbf63c5 and opened as `index.html?snap&qa` with merging off. The probes are Playwright scripts that read `__mv` and cast rays against the triangles in the movement's frame. On that frame the pillar plate's dial face is y 3.35, the dial is 10.35-11.65 and the cannon tube's crown is 18.255.
My crops and scripts are in the session scratchpad `dc2/b7r/` (`crop.py`, `probe.py`, `p1.js`-`p6.js`).

Unless a row says otherwise, scale readings are taken on the sheet's own pixels. 05 02_1 reads about 11.87 px/mm near the centre: 23 mm between the centre and the up-and-down arbor spans 273 px, and the hands' figures 2, 3.2 and 3.5 read 11.8-12.0. Elsewhere on that sheet the scale is not uniform (the plate's 3.9 scales about 10 px/mm).

## Extra checks: ten figures most likely misread

1. **The dial's thickness, 05 02_12: the figure is struck and corrected.** "Stärke 1,3" has a stroke through "1,3" and "1,2" written above it (crop `d5.png`). The title block has the same: "Messingblech 1,3" struck, with "1,2" over it (`d4.png`). The stock is "Rohmaß 1,3×110×110" and the sheet says "plan gespannt". **The model draws the dial 1.3** (`DT = 1.3`; mesh 10.35-11.65). Note that 05 02_1 still figures 1,3. This could be a new finding; likely the finished dial is 1.2, faced from 1.3 stock. Moving the dial top down 0.1 would move every "over the dial" height in the model.
2. **The minute hand's hole is square** (□2,6, not Ø 2,6). The model's is square (half-side 1.311 at the top). Correct.
3. **The minute hand's 1,2 against its 2**: 1.2 is the hand's top to the flange's underside, 2 the whole. Model: 15.7 / 16.5 / 17.7. Correct.
4. **The hour hand's Ø 5 −0,2** is the pipe's diameter with its tolerance (crop `hh2.png`: "5⌀" over "−0,2"), not a separate 0.2. The model takes 4.8, the low limit; the middle would be 4.9. Minor.
5. **The up-and-down hand's 11,7 and 9,5** both end on the same line, where the inked blade ends. 11.7 starts from the boss's far edge: 9.5 + 2.2 (Ø 4.4 / 2). The sheet gives no length for the fine tip beyond. The model's 10.2, taken from a photograph, is the only source. Correct.
6. **05 02 04's 1,4 / 0,7 / 2,8 / 5,2**: seat 1.4, flange 0.7 over the wheel, pipe top 2.8 over the wheel's top, 5.2 under the wheel. Model: yw0 8.55 (5.2), flange top 10.25 (0.7), step to 10.95, top 12.35 (2.8 over 9.55, seat 1.4). Correct. The flange scales Ø 7.8 (17 px/mm by the wheel's 1.0) against the model's 7.7. Fine.
7. **05 02 02's 7 −0,1 / 4,3 +0,1 / 0,9 +0,2 / 3,05 −0,04**: the model's pipe is 6.9 (low limit), the pinion's lower face 4.3 under the pipe's top, the foot 1.0 under the wheel (middle of 0.9-1.1), Ø 3.05 (the upper limit). Correct.
8. **05 02 03's 8,2 −0,1 overall**: the model's 6.615-14.815 is 8.2, the upper limit. Correct.
9. **The dial's sinks, "H7 1,8 +0,009" boxed with "Senkung 1,0 tief"**: the model's sinks are Ø 1.8 × 1.0 deep over Ø 1.1 holes on Ø 101, and the dial is Ø 103.55 against "fertig 103,6 −0,1". Correct.
10. **The seconds hand's hole "1,2 +0,05"** and boss Ø 3,2: the model's hole is 1.2 on the collet's 1.2 end, line to line (−0.0005 to +0.0024). Correct.

## Disagreements after reading batch7-model.md

I agree with M1, M2, M3, M4, M6 and M7, and with the F3, F7 and F8 settlements. They are the same verdicts reached independently, on the same numbers:
- M1: 0.010-0.0126 on the hour hand, 0.011-0.023 on the minute hand's flats, 0.009-0.011 on the up-and-down hand.
- M3: the ring's wall at r 0.518-0.520 at every height, against the screw's helix at 0.349-0.487.
- M4: the blade on rows 137-140 under a flange on rows 135-140.
- F8: 05 02_1's own collar 3.6-3.7 over the wheel.

I disagree with three points, and add a nuance to a fourth.

1. **M5, the claim that the 1943 tube bounds the model's hand heights. I disagree.** batch7-model.md accepts that 05 02_1 draws the hands about 0.95 lower. But it keeps the model's heights on the argument that "the hour hand can come down 0.385 at most (its top flush with its seat), and the minute hand then only to 0.05 over it". Nothing requires the hour hand's top to be flush with its seat.
   - The hand is pressed on a 1:70 taper, so its height is wherever the press stops. Lowering it 0.95 asks the hole to be 0.0136 smaller on the diameter, inside the sheets' 0.003-0.023 band. The hour wheel's pipe then stands up through the hand.
   - That is how 05 02_1 draws it: the pipe hatched up past the hour hand's top to just under the minute hand's boss (`ar2.png`).
   - The minute hand can come down until its square hole meets the shoulder where the tube's Ø 3.6 ends and the square begins: yC + 10.8 = 14.865, which is **3.215 over the dial face**. The boxed **3,2** is that shoulder.
   - With the hour hand's top at the boxed **2,6** (14.25), the hour seat's top (14.815) is 0.05 under the minute hand, as the model's own 0.05 rule wants.
   - Both are boxed figures ("Eingerahmte Maße werden bei der Abnahme besonders geprüft"): heights checked at acceptance.
   - So the arrangement's heights fit the model's own 1943 parts exactly, and the model's are not forced. I checked what else moves: the hour hand's pipe foot comes to 0.5 inside the dial's Ø 6 hole (r 2.4 against 3.0, clear) and stays 0.8 over the hour wheel's collar. Its blade is 1.8 over the dial, still 1.1 over the seconds hand's top and over the up-and-down hand (0.7).
   - The correction is geometry, not only the text: the hour hand's top at 2.6 and the minute hand on the square's shoulder at 3.2 over the dial, in every version. The minute hand then sits down on the shoulder; the square's 2.6 □ r6 press doesn't set its height.
2. **M10 / C10, the model's glass.** batch7-model.md gives the glass "3.7 thick ... its underside about 15.0 over the plate face" from case.js's comments and says it wasn't measured. Measured on the page at efbf63c5 (Dial view, the movement in the case), the glass 050101-1 is **3.5 thick (18.95-22.45), its underside 15.60 over the plate's dial face**. That is case.js line 516's b0 + 12.3 to + 15.8, set by batch 10 of the first check on 26 October 2026. It is 1.25 over the minute hand and 0.7 over the tube's crown. The 3.7 is case.js line 497's stale comment. Against 05 02_1, the model is 0.2 under the inked 15,8 and 1.4 under the pencil 17,0, and 0.5 over "Glas 3" (which the sheet itself draws about 4.0 thick). I also read 15,8 as inked and not struck: the pencil dashed leader crosses it, but the figure has no strike of its own (`ar4.png`). That one is possible either way.
3. **The dial's thickness, rated "match (the iron revision 1.3, README)".** On 05 02_12, both "Stärke 1,3" and the title block's "Messingblech 1,3" carry a stroke with "1,2" written over them (`d5.png`, `d4.png`). The "Eisenblech 1,3 gebondert" note below is a separate revision. So the sheet gives the brass dial **1.2** and the iron dial 1.3. The model draws **1.3 at brass's density (8.45)**: the iron revision's thickness with the brass revision's material, which matches neither. Either revision is defensible as a choice, but the mix isn't: brass at 1.2 moves the dial's face and every "over the dial" height 0.1 down; iron at 1.3 changes only the weight. Undecidable which revision to build, but the inconsistency is sure.
4. **The collet (J17), "holds as an estimate".** It is drawn 0.0033-0.0048 clear on the post's 1:90, yet its bom line says "Pressed". Since seatcheck never follows it (M6), I count it with M1: in the model, none of the four hands and not the collet is held by anything. That a press is drawn as clearance is a fact on the solid, whatever the estimate. A line-to-line or slightly tight bore would match the model's press convention elsewhere (the up-and-down pinion's 0.025).
