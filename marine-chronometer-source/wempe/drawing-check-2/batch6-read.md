1. **F1 (inconsistent treatment; 05-05_02, _05, _08 against train.js; likely).** The three assembly sheets draw the same feature at each wheel's hub, a "0,2" step and a diameter (6, 7, 10 Ø) a little under each wheel's hub (6,5, 7,5, 10,6), and each wheel's stock is 0,2 thicker than its finished rim. train.js builds the centre wheel's hub 0.2 proud, turned to Ø 10, but the intermediate and seconds wheels' hubs flush ("the riveting's sink"). The sheets (one elevation each, s02m, s05a, s08i) don't settle which; photographs of the train wheels' hubs would.
