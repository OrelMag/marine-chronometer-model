# Batch 10, blind reading: the case, gimbals, boxes, keys, handle, maker's plate and latch tongue (05-01 and 51-00 sheets)

Read 28 October 2026 from the sheet images alone (`References/wempe/drawings/05-01/Wempe_05-01_{1..9}.jpg`, `51-00/Wempe_51-00_{1..5}.jpg`), with the coloured section (`Wempe_Einheitschronometer.jpg`) and the movement ring (`05-08/Wempe_05-08_07.jpg`) only for how the case holds the movement. Steps 1 and 2 were written for every sheet before any transcription, README or code was opened. Crops are in the session scratchpad, `dc2/b10/` (`s1*` for 05-01_1, `s2*` for 05-01_2 and so on, `u1*`-`u5*` for 51-00_1-5, `c1`, `c2` for the coloured section).

The scans are small (1024 px on the long side). The A3 sheets at 1:1 come out about 2.44 px per mm, the A4 sheets about 3.55 px per mm, so on the 2:1 sheets a millimetre of the part is about 7 px. Most figures read cleanly once enlarged; where one does not I say so. "Drawn" sizes below are scaled off the scan as a check on a reading, never as a reading in their own right.

The sheets fall into four groups, which I keep apart:

- **The Einheits case** (05 01, the gimbal assembly of 1940, and its parts drawn 1941-42): 05-01_1 to _5, 05-01_8, _9, and 51-00_3 (the maker's plate).
- **The winding keys**, two different designs under one number 51 00 11: the key of December 1940 (05-01_6, "127-556.02 U 01", screw-held cap, pear-shaped grip) and the key of July 1943 (05-01_7, cross pin, round grip), whose middle part and pipe are 05-01_8 and _9.
- **The 127-556 "Boden-Chronometer" boxes** (51-00_1, 51-00_5): another clock's general arrangement (a dial with small seconds at 6 and no up-and-down dial, a Buna rubber case, a hand-setting knob and a balance-holding device) and its padded outer box.
- **The wooden box's fittings**: the handle (51-00_2, "Änderung von 510010") and the latch tongue (51-00_4, P. 51 0002-2).

- **Not drawn anywhere on this sheet:** a size for any part but the box; how the bezel is held on the bowl (no thread, no screw); the bowl's floor hole and the winding hole (see 05-01_2, _3).
