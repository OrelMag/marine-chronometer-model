### Interpretation

001 is the movement's general layout at 2:1: the two plates Ø 85, 19 mm apart inside, the case's inner Ø 100, an optional antimagnetic liner, the plan places of barrel, fusee, balance, escape wheel, detent, cocks, bridges and their screws. It fixes positions in plan and which parts lie on which side, not sizes, heights or how the screws are threaded.
