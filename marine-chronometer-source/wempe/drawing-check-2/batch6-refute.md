# Batch 6, refutation: the going train (05 05_01 to 05 05_10)

Refuter, step 7 of PLAN-wempe-drawing-check-2.md, 28 October 2026. I wrote my verdicts below before I read `batch6-model.md`. I never opened `batch6-read.md`, the transcriptions or the first pass's reports. I read the code comments in `wempe/js/train.js` only to learn what was intended.

- **Sheets.** 05 05_01 to _10, 05 08_10 (Zwischenbrücke kompl.), 05 02_01 and the coloured section `Wempe_Einheitschronometer.jpg`. They were cropped and enlarged with PIL. The crops are in the scratchpad `dc2\b6r\` (`s02_*`, `s04_*`, `s05_*`, `s07_*`, `s08_*`, `s10_*`, `s01_*`, `s0201_*`, `cs_sec.png`, `cs_ctr.png`). At 5:1 the portrait scans are about 17.2 px per model mm.
- **Model.** The read-only worktree `wempe-check2-audit2` (main 28ab3a44), opened as `index.html?snap&qa` on the GPU with Performance merging off and `update()` stubbed. Two scripts did the measuring:
  - `b6r\probe.py` rings every visible mesh about its nearest train arbor, giving the radius bins along y (`probe_last.json`).
  - `b6r\col.py` casts rays both ways along each arbor's axis at 14 radii, in `last`, `first`, `1943`, `6mx` and `3955` (`col_*.json`).
  - y is measured from each arbor's group origin; −y is toward the dial.
- **Photograph.** `dg-chrono-dec/8_Wempe_3955_Räderwerk_plus.jpg`, the train seen from the top-plate side with the top plate off, cropped at the hubs (`p8_sec.png`, `p8_ctr.png`).

## Disagreements after reading batch6-model.md

I agree with M3 (C3), M4 (C4), M6, M7, M8 and M9. Where I differ:

1. **M1 (C1): what the "0,2" marks.**
   - **batch6-model.md's reading:** each kompl. sheet draws "the hub's face 0,2 off the rim's", and its row for 05 05_8 calls the model's 0.25-proud centre boss a match.
   - **My reading:** on all three kompl. sheets the hub's face and the rim's face are drawn on one line. The 0,2 is measured to a line *inside the hub's section*, its extension line starting at the hub. On _8 the apparent 0.15 step is the scan's skew: the bottom line falls by the same amount over the same width.
   - **Other evidence:** the coloured section, which they don't cite for this, draws all three hubs flush with their rims. The 0,2 could equally be the riveting's sink (05 05_5's "7 Ø vernietet" points at it). Only the stock-thickness argument and Wempe 3955's seconds wheel support a proud hub.
   - **Where we agree:** the three are built differently while the sheets draw them alike. Their correction (all three alike) stands either way.
   - **What I'd change:** which way they should all go (all proud, as 3955 shows, or all flush, as the sheets and coloured section draw) should be recorded as two readings for the owner (O-3). M1 shouldn't assume proud. Their 05 05_8 row ("match") should become "one reading of two".
2. **M3's sources (C3).** They say 05 02_1 draws the seconds hand at the 7,2 post's height. 05 02_1 doesn't show the seconds arbor at all. The evidence that does settle it is 05 05_01's "0,5" from the dial's face to the post's tip (`s01_dial.png`), which neither the claim nor M3 cites, together with the coloured section. My verdict stands (likely), on that basis.
3. **M10 / J15 (C10).** "Relation missing" holds only in letter. The jewel's seating in 050504-2 is checked by bom-check.js's jewel rays, as it is for every jewel of both models, none of which carries a `rel`. I'd close it as "no action", or at most add a `press` relation for uniformity with the setting's.
4. **M9: an addition, not a disagreement.** The short intermediate seat also takes the sheet's 4,6 −0,2 (seat and leaves) out of tolerance: 4.20 against 4.4-4.6. Their row 106 judged the leaves alone (3,3) and calls it a match.

No row I tried broke beyond these.
