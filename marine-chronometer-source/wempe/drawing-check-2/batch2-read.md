Sources: `References/wempe/drawings/05-04/` (the nine sheets), `Wempe_Einheitschronometer.jpg` (the coloured section) and `001-002/Wempe_001.jpg` (joints only). Convention noted on these sheets (it matters for the stud lesson): a diameter may be dimensioned from a centreline to one edge with a single arrow (05-04_01's "8ø" runs from the balance staff's centreline to the edge of the top plate's opening: half the dimension line, a diameter's figure). And on 05 04 01-2 "1,2" runs from a hole's centreline to the end edge, a distance, not a slot or a hole size.
