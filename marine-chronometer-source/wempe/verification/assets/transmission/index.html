What moves

The barrel turns on its mainspring and pulls the chain off the fusee. The fusee drives the maintaining ratchet through its two clicks, the ratchet drives the fusee wheel through the maintaining spring, and the fusee wheel drives the train to the escape wheel, which the balance lets go a tooth each half second. The cannon pinion carries the minute hand and turns the motion work; a pinion on the fusee arbor turns the up-and-down wheel.

How it is computed

Each part is a body with its inertia weighed on the model's solids. Every mesh is worked tooth by tooth on the page's own outlines, with its backlash and friction; the chain pulls but can go slack; the clicks and the detent ride their teeth; the mainspring's coils stick and slip. The escapement is dynamics.js's own, driven by the train each step.

Reading the colours

Grey is a part at its running load at 12 hours; blue is less, red is more, on a log scale. The motion work and the up-and-down work carry only friction, so they are coloured against the most they carry in the scenario. Rotations are drawn as computed; only the elastic deviations are scaled.
