# Hamilton Model 21: original factory drawings (NAWCC)

Scans of Hamilton Watch Co. (Lancaster, Pa.) engineering drawings for the Model 21 marine chronometer, received from the NAWCC (inventoried 31 October 2026). Each PDF is one page, one 300 dpi JPEG scan, with the NAWCC's text overlay **"Not to be reproduced without the permission of the NAWCC."** Like the NAWCC Bulletin articles (`References/README.md`), they are the owner's copies: not to be committed to the public repository, put on the site, or reproduced in the page, the reports or the essay. Their numbers may be cited.

All dimensions on the sheets are in inches, tolerances "in units of the last decimal place" (so `.0250 +0 −5` is 0.0250 +0/−0.0005 in). Every sheet is signed "E.W.D." (approved or checked) unless noted. Each one's title-block code `NNNNN-S-RR` is the part number, the sheet size (1 = 8½×11 in, 2 = 11×17 in) and the revision or drawing type (`01` the finished part; `51A`, `52` the operation drawings).

The "Parts list" column gives the line in the manual's parts list (`marine-chronometer-source/chronometer-working-model/bom.json`, Sec. XI). Sub-parts that the manual doesn't list on their own say "not listed".

## Each drawing

### Mainspring and barrel

**`1-WD-265.pdf`: Proposed Marine Chronometer Mainspring.** The inner terminal (the eye end) of the mainspring, proposed 5-20-43, drawn K.R.A., 2:1. The strip is .590 ±.001 wide (15.0 mm). The end is rounded at .250 R, with a slot .200 ±.003 wide, starting .200 from the end and .340 long; the end of the slot is 1.25 from the tip. The note reads "Proposed inner terminal for marine chronometer mainsprings. For all other dimensions see drawing #42037 to 041-3-01." Pencilled metric notes: 31.8 (for 1.25), 5.10 and .180 (written over the circled .200), 6.35 R (for .250 R). In blue ink: "Finished drawings revised by D.C.M. of 6-7-43", the part number struck through. This is the only sheet in the set on the mainspring itself. Its other sizes (thickness, length) are on drawings 42037-42041, which are not in the set.

**`42168-2-01.pdf`: Mainspring Barrel, finished.** In section and plan. OD 1.710 ±.003, bore 1.500 +.002, overall height .700 ±.002, bottom .060 ±.001. The lip for the cap is 1.650 ±.003 × .080. The centre boss has a hole of .2300 +.003 (.320 and .340 steps, 30°/45°/60° chamfers). Tapped for five .0340-130 screws, close fit, equally spaced. The anchor pin is rivet part #42042, .080 dia, finished flush with the barrel, its straight side vertical and facing the way shown. There is a .055 hole at .520 ±.010 radius and a .090 lip. The outside and inside diameters must be concentric with the centre hole. Cast yellow brass, 24K gold plate. Drawn J.M.G., 9-10-41, memos to 4-30-43.

**`42169-2-01.pdf`: Barrel Cap, finished.** OD 1.650 ±.003, spigot 1.500 +0/−.001 (concentric with the centre hole), plate .060 ±.001, rim .0360 ±.005 and .058, boss .100 +.001, centre hole .2300 +.003 (steps .320/.340, chamfers 30°/45°/60°). Five holes equally spaced on 1.575. Rolled yellow brass, Scovill alloy #187, 24K gold plate; break all corners. Drawn K.R.A., 8-29-41, memos to 1-30-43.

**`42170-2-01.pdf`: Barrel Arbor, finished.** Overall 1.59 ±.001. Bearings .1695 +0/−.003 and .2295 +0/−.003, polished. Body .400 dia between collars .530 and .720. The square is .110 +.004 (.145 dia shank). The hook is .120 ±.002 wide, at .325. The spring collar is .520 dia with a "start spiral" (1.00 R, .022 step, 30° and 45°, .090/.150/.210 sizes). Steel, S.A.E. "10120" as written (perhaps 1020). Drawn K.R.A., checked B.L.H., marked redrawn, memos 10-22-43 and 4-29-44.

## Numbers that cross-check the model

Read off the sheets and compared with what the model already uses. Nothing in the model has been changed from these drawings yet.

- **Scale:** every absolute size on the drawings is 1.11-1.16 times the model's (the fusee wheel 40.97 against 36.06 mm over the tips, the train's centre distances, the ratchets, the barrel), while the ratios between parts agree. `PLAN-hamilton-drawings.md` (root), Phase 0, has the table and what would settle it.

- Train: fusee wheel 90 teeth, fourth wheel 75, fourth pinion 10, as `TRAIN` in `js/movement.js` (`fu:90, fw:75, fp:10`). The modules are new: the fusee wheel 0.44 mm, the fourth wheel and pinion 0.28 mm.
- Ratchets: sustaining 120 teeth (60°), winding 40 (65°), set-up 40 (65°). The winding and set-up ratchets are .670 in (17.0 mm) over the tips.
- Chain: pitch .0940 in (2.388 mm), 297 spaces (27.92 in, 709 mm), links .0095 in (0.24 mm) thick, pins .024 in (0.61 mm).
- Barrel: OD 1.710 in (43.43 mm), bore 1.500 (38.10), height .700 (17.78), five cap screws .0340-130. Mainspring width .590 (14.99 mm).
- Thicknesses: sustaining ratchet wheel .050 in, fusee wheel plate .045, fourth wheel .035, winding pawl .050, sustaining pawl .045, set-up ratchet .048, sustaining spring .050, detent blank .045 ground to .041.
- Materials the manual doesn't give: the pawl springs are beryllium copper; the barrel cast yellow brass; the cap Scovill #187; the set-up ratchet SAE 1095; most steel parts "1.00 carbon steel"; finishes 24K gold plate, nickel plate or oxidized black.
