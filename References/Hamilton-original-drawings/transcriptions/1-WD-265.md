# 1-WD-265 Proposed Marine Chronometer Mainspring, inner terminal (transcription)

Source: References/Hamilton-original-drawings/1-WD-265.pdf (not in the repository). Portrait sheet, legible.

## Title block
- PROPOSED MARINE CHRONOMETER MAINSPRING. Part no. 1-WD-265 (struck through in blue pencil). Material blank. Scale 2:1. Drawn K.R.A., checked E.W.D. Date 5-20-43.
- Note verbatim: "PROPOSED INNER TERMINAL FOR MARINE CHRONOMETER MAINSPRINGS. FOR ALL OTHER DIMENSIONS SEE DRAWING #42037 TO 041-3-01." Also "BREAK CORNER" and ".250 R.".
- Blue ink: "Finished drawings revised by D.C.M. of 6-7-43" with initials E.W.D.
- Later pencil notes (not original): "31.8" over a struck-out "31.75" (for 1.25), "5.10" beside .200, a blue ".180" above the circled .200, "6.35 R." under .250 R.

## What it shows
The inner end of the strip in plan, broken off at the right: the strip narrows towards a full-round end and has an oblong slot (round-ended) near the tip.

## Items
1. Strip width 0.590 in = 14.9860 mm +-.001. Sure.
2. End radius 0.250 in = 6.3500 mm R. Sure (the end is a full round of half the end width 0.500 in).
3. Length of the taper 1.25 in = 31.7500 mm from the tip to where the full width begins (witness line at the right end of the dimension). Sure for the number; the point it ends at is likely. Taper: width .500 at the tip to .590, derived from the outline (0.090 in = 2.286 mm in total, 1.143 each side). Likely.
4. Slot width 0.200 in = 5.0800 mm +-.003 (across the strip). Sure.
5. Slot start from the tip: 0.200 in = 5.0800 mm (printed, circled in blue) with the blue handwritten ".180" above it. Printed value .200 sure; .180 is a later hand change.
6. Slot length 0.340 in = 8.6360 mm (start to end), ends round (radius .100). Sure. Slot end from the tip .540 (.520 with .180).
7. Corner break at the slot's inner end.

## What it tells the model
- Strip width 14.986 mm (British Museum 15.0). The model's strip thickness t = 0.4401 mm (0.394*1.117); the manual gives 0.0165 in = 0.4191 mm. This sheet gives no thickness.
- The slot slides over the arbor's hook: the arbor's hook slot is .120 wide (42170) and this slot .200: about .080 in clearance.
- Tip length 1.25 in = 31.750 mm (31.8 pencilled): compare the model's eye in mainspring.py (MC_MS_EYE), not examined here.

## Hard to read
- #5: .200 printed against .180 handwritten, both legible.
- #3: taper read from the outline, not stated; about +-0.01 in.

## Verification, 1 November 2026 (crops at 110-600 dpi, read with the scan open; images kept outside the repository)

Read on a 110 dpi crop of the portrait sheet (scale from the .590: 219 px/in).
- Item 3 (the 1.25): **settled, sure.** It runs from the tip to the end of the taper (the witness line); the strip is .500 wide where the end round meets it, and widens by the straight taper to .590 at 1.25 from the tip.
- Items 5, 6 (the slot): **settled.** The slot is .200 +-.003 wide, .340 long; its tip end is **square with a broken corner**, its far end a full round (R .100) (the first reading had both ends round). The slot starts .200 from the tip as printed (circled), changed by hand to .180 (blue ink, with the note "Finished drawings revised by D.C.M. of 6-7-43"): the sheet is dated 5-20-43, so .180 is the later change and likely the one in force; .200 the original. Both are read sure; which one the springs followed the sheet does not settle.
- Tolerances: .590 +-.001; .200 +-.003.
