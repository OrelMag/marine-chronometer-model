# 42169-2-01 Barrel Cap, finished (transcription)

Source: References/Hamilton-original-drawings/42169-2-01.pdf (not in the repository). Legible at 300 dpi. Tolerances in units of the last decimal place.

## Title block
- BARREL CAP, FINISHED. Part no. 42169-2-01. Material block "AS NOTED"; note "MATERIAL: ROLLED YELLOW BRASS SCOVILL ALLOY #187." and "24 K. GOLD PLATE." Scale 5:1. Drawn K.R.A., checked (E.W.D.). Sheet letter M.
- Dates: 8-29-41; memos 11-11-41, 2-18-42, 1-30-43 (no memo text).
- Notes verbatim: "5 HOLES EQUALLY SPACED"; "THIS DIAMETER MUST BE CONCENTRIC WITH CENTER HOLE." (leader to the 1.500 spigot diameter); "BREAK ALL CORNERS"; "CORNER".

## What it shows
Left: a half plan (half disc) with the 5-hole circle (3 holes visible, each two concentric circles) and the centre boss rings. Centre: a section with the plate vertical, a thin raised rim (spigot) at its outer edge and the centre boss.

## Items
1. Outside diameter 1.650 in = 41.9100 mm +-.003. Sure.
2. Spigot diameter 1.500 in = 38.1000 mm +0/-.001, concentric with the centre hole. Sure.
3. Pitch circle of the 5 holes 1.575 in = 40.0050 mm, 5 equally spaced (72 deg). Sure. Hole size not dimensioned.
4. Plate thickness 0.060 in = 1.5240 mm +-.001. Sure.
5. Rim (spigot) width 0.0360 in = 0.9144 mm +-.005, with heights 0.058 in = 1.4732 mm (overall from the rim's end to the plate's outer-edge face) and 0.030 in = 0.7620 mm +-.001 (the plate edge's outer height step at the bottom). The rim's outer edge has a 45 deg chamfer. Likely (see Hard to read).
6. Boss height 0.100 in = 2.5400 mm +.001/-0 from the plate's outer face. Likely.
7. Centre hole 0.2300 in = 5.8420 mm +.003/-0. Sure.
8. Boss diameters 0.320 in = 8.1280 mm and 0.340 in = 8.6360 mm; cones 60 deg, 45 deg and 30 deg as in 42168; 0.025 in = 0.6350 mm and 0.010 in = 0.2540 mm small steps at the hole. Same arrangement as the barrel's centre. Likely.
9. "BREAK ALL CORNERS" (no radius given).

## What it tells the model
- Cap OD 41.910 mm, spigot 38.100 mm (equals the barrel's inside diameter), plate 1.524 mm, boss 2.540 mm from the outer face. Compare the model's cap (not changed here).
- Bores of the barrel and cap are the same, 0.2300 in = 5.8420 mm+.003, over the arbor's bearing 0.2295 in = 5.8293 mm+0/-.003: a clearance of 0.0005-0.0038 in (0.013-0.097 mm).

## Hard to read
- #5: .058, .0360 +-.005 and .030 +-.001 near the rim: digits clear, the edges each spans inferred, likely.
- #6 and #8: assignment of .100, .320 and .340: likely.
- Hole and counterbore sizes of the 5 holes are not given.
