# 42168-2-01 Mainspring Barrel, finished (transcription)

Source: References/Hamilton-original-drawings/42168-2-01.pdf (NAWCC scan, not in the repository). Read at 300 dpi. Printed note: all dimensions in inches, tolerances in units of the last decimal place (".700 +-2" means +-.002).

## Title block
- MAINSPRING BARREL, FINISHED. Part no. 42168-2-01. Material: CAST YELLOW BRASS. Finish: 24 K. GOLD PLATE. Scale 3:1. Drawn J.M.G.; checked (initials E.W.D.). Sheet letter M.
- Dates: 9-10-41 (original); memos 11-11-41, 12-26-41, 1-30-43, 4-30-43 (dates only, no memo text on the sheet).
- Notes verbatim: "TAP FOR .0340-130 SCREW, CLOSE FIT. 5 HOLES EQUALLY SPACED."; "OUTSIDE AND INSIDE DIAMETERS OF BARREL MUST BE CONCENTRIC WITH CENTER HOLE."; ".080 DIA. RIVET PART #42042 FINISH FLUSH WITH SURFACE OF BARREL."; "STRAIGHT SIDE OF ANCHOR PIN MUST BE VERTICAL AND FACE IN DIRECTION SHOWN."; two "CORNER" leaders (one on the boss profile, one on the wall notch in plan).

## What it shows
Left: a section through the axis, closed floor on the left, wall rising to the right, open end at the right with a stepped lip carrying the screw holes; a tapped hole with a screw drawn in the lip. Right: plan of the open side: the 5 tapped holes on a dashed circle, the centre boss rings, a part-section of the wall showing the anchor pin and a notch. Below: an enlarged detail (wall broken off both sides) of the anchor pin seen from inside, with a second small hole at lower left.

## Items
1. Overall height 0.700 in = 17.7800 mm +-.002. Sure.
2. Lip step: 0.080 in = 2.0320 mm at the open end (the end of the wall is stepped down in diameter to carry the cap). Sure.
3. Floor thickness 0.060 in = 1.5240 mm +-.001. Sure.
4. Outside diameter of the barrel 1.710 in = 43.4340 mm +-.003. Sure.
5. Lip outside diameter 1.650 in = 41.9100 mm +-.003. Sure. Radial step 0.030 in = 0.7620 mm (derived).
6. Inside diameter 1.500 in = 38.1000 mm +.002/-0. Sure. Wall thickness (1.710-1.500)/2 = 0.105 in = 2.6670 mm (derived).
7. Pitch circle of the 5 tapped holes 1.575 in = 40.0050 mm diameter (dimension line in plan, across the dashed circle). Sure.
8. Five holes, tapped for the 0.0340 in = 0.8636 mm-130 screw (0.0340 in major diameter; 130 threads/in = pitch 0.1954 mm), close fit, equally spaced (72 deg). Sure.
9. Centre boss on the floor's inside: height ".100 +.002/-0" = 0.100 in = 2.5400 mm measured from the floor's outside face (so the boss stands 0.100-0.060 = 0.040 in = 1.0160 mm above the floor's inside face). Likely.
10. Centre hole 0.2300 in = 5.8420 mm +.0003/-0. Sure.
11. Boss/hole profile diameters: 0.320 in = 8.1280 mm and 0.340 in = 8.6360 mm. Likely .340 is the boss's top-face diameter and .320 the diameter of the conical step on the outside face; the sheet draws 30 deg (outer cone at the hole's outside edge), 45 deg (boss's top edge, inside) and 60 deg (at the lower side where the boss meets the floor). Angles sure, which diameter belongs to which cone likely.
12. 0.010 in = 0.2540 mm small land at the hole's edge ("CORNER" leader: a sharp/broken corner) and 0.025 in = 0.6350 mm floor step beside the boss base. Likely.
13. Anchor pin: rivet part #42042, 0.080 in = 2.0320 mm dia, finished flush with the barrel's surface, on the plan's vertical axis below the centre at radius 0.520 in = 13.2080 mm +-.010 from the centre (".520 +-10"). The straight (flat) side of the pin must be vertical and face as the detail shows. Sure for size and radius.
14. Small hole 0.055 in = 1.3970 mm dia +0/-.003 (".055 +0/-3 DIA.") in the floor at about 8 o'clock; its distance from the outside face of the floor is 0.140 in = 3.5560 mm in the section view. Likely (the .140 may instead locate the anchor pin hole; see Hard to read).
15. 0.365 in = 9.2710 mm from the floor's outside face to the anchor pin's axis in the section. Likely.
16. 0.090 in = 2.2860 mm +0/-.005 (".090 +0/-5"): the width of the lip step measured at the lower right of the section (at the open end, the dimension is drawn beside the 0.080 in = 2.0320 mm at the top). Likely.
17. Notch in the inner wall at about 8 o'clock (the "CORNER" leader in plan): drawn as a small rectangular notch, no size given. Its purpose (a catch or a spring tail seat) is not stated.

## What it tells the model
- Barrel: OD 43.434 mm (r 21.717), inside diameter 38.100 mm (r 19.05), wall 2.667, height 17.780, floor 1.524, lip 2.032 high. The model's barrel inside radius is 18.3*1.117 = 20.44 (mainspring.py Rw 20.4411 less brace) and its wall 2.234 (2.0*1.117): the drawn bore is 38.1 mm (matches the British Museum's 38 mm), so the model's barrel is about 7 % wider inside and thinner walled than drawn; the 1.117 rescale does not fix it (the barrel is not a plate-scaled size). Reported only.
- Barrel height 17.78 against the strip 14.986 wide: 2.8 mm spare across floor-to-cap clearance.
- Anchor pin radius 13.208 mm, dia 2.032 mm.

## Hard to read
- #9, #11: which of .320/.340 goes with which cone; whether .100 runs from the floor's outside face (likely). The profile is a stepped bore with three bevels in the section's left edge.
- #12 .010 and .025: tiny steps, digits clear, assignment likely.
- #14, #15: .140 and .365 digits are clear; what they locate (the .055 hole and the anchor pin) is likely, not sure.
- #17: no dimensions for the notch; no radius for the small hole in plan.

## Verification, 1 November 2026 (crops at 110-600 dpi, read with the scan open; images kept outside the repository)

Read on a 130 dpi crop of the whole sheet (scale from the .700: 393 px/in).
- Items 9, 11 (.100, .320, .340 and the cones): **settled, likely.** .320 is the diameter of the chamfer's edge on the outer face (a cone from the .2300 bore out to .320, .025 deep: 30 deg to the face = 60 deg to the axis, the two angles 30 and 60 are the same cone; .045 radial over .025 deep checks); .340 is the diameter of the boss's flat top on the inside (a 45 deg cone from the floor up to it); the .010 land is the bore's corner on the inside.
- Items 14, 15 (.140, .365): **settled, sure.** Both are axial positions along the wall, from the floor's outside face: the small .055 +0/-.003 hole's centre at .140 and the anchor rivet's at .365.
- Item 2 against item 16 (the lip: .080 at the top and .090 +0/-.005 at the bottom of the section): the sheet says both, and the lip is drawn the same size at both ends. **Contradiction within the sheet; unsure.** .080 (the first, printed with the lip step) stays until 42169's spigot depth is read against it.
- Item 12 (.025): the .025 dimension at the floor's inside near the lower edge is not resolved (arrows at the floor's inside face); unsure. It changes nothing the model draws.
- Item 17 (the notch) and item 13: the notch and the small hole sit at about 8 o'clock; the rivet at the bottom of the plan, .520 +-.010 from the centre.
- Correction: item 10's bore is .2300 +.0003/-0 (four decimals: the last place is .0001).
- Tolerances by the last-place rule: .700 +-.002; .060 +-.001; .100 +.002/-0; 1.710 +-.003; 1.650 +-.003; 1.500 +.002/-0; .2300 +.0003/-0; .520 +-.010; .055 +0/-.003; .090 +0/-.005.
