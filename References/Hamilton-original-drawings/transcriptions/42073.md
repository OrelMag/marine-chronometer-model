## What it tells the model

- TRAIN fw=75, fp=10 agrees with 75 teeth and 10 leaves.
- Drawn OD .8566 in = 21.7576 mm against the nominal m(z+2) = 21.56 mm (module .28): about 0.2 mm over, so tip allowance is generous; compare the model fourth wheel OD.
- Rim inside .700 in = 17.780 mm, hub .290 in = 7.366 mm, thickness .035 in = 0.889 mm.

## Hard to read

- .037 versus .048: both sit on the horizontal spoke; .037 is the arrow pair at the rim end, .048 near the hub (likely).
- Which of 75 or 72 belongs to which gap: 75 upper, 72 lower (likely).
- The .100: its arrows point to the pinion body step in the side view (likely).

## Verification, 1 November 2026 (crops at 110-600 dpi, read with the scan open; images kept outside the repository)

- Item 7/8 (spoke widths): **settled, sure.** The spoke tapers: .037 at the rim end, .048 at the hub end (the .037's arrows span the spoke at the rim, the .048's at the hub; the two edges converge towards the rim). Width .037 = 0.9398 mm, .048 = 1.2192 mm.
- Items 9/10 (75 deg, 72 deg): **settled, sure.** Both angles are between spoke centre lines (dash-dot) measured from the horizontal spoke (the one on the left): the next spoke up is 75 deg round, the next down 72 deg round. The wheel is therefore not at an even 72 deg pitch about that spoke. The two spokes in the half not drawn are not dimensioned: the remaining 213 deg between them is not given (likely 71 deg each by symmetry; unsure, not drawn).
- Item 12 (.100): likely, unchanged (arrows at the pinion body's left end and the wheel face).
- Tolerances by the last-place rule: .8566 +.0010/-.0020; .035 +0/-.002.
