Source: `References/Hamilton-original-drawings/42073-2-01-01.pdf` (one sheet; the scan is not reproduced).

## Title block
- Title: Fourth Wheel, complete with pinion
- Part no.: 42073-2-01-01
- Material: Brass
- Finish: 24K. GOLD PLATE WHEEL ONLY
- Scale: 5:1
- Dates / memos (verbatim): 8-1-41; REVISED 8-11-41; REVISED 8-27-41; MEMO. 11-24-41; MEMO. 4-19-43
- Drawn / checked: K.R.A. / checked E.W.D. (handwritten)
- Sheet note: "ALL DIMENSIONS ARE GIVEN IN INCHES. ALL TOLERANCES SHOWN ARE IN UNITS OF THE LAST DECIMAL PLACE" (so +10/-20 on .8566 means +.0010/-.0020).

## What the sheet shows
Left: a half plan of the wheel with a wavy tooth sketch on the rim, a thin rim, five spokes meeting a hub with the pinion leaves at the centre. Right: a side section of the wheel on the pinion with the rivet called out. Very faint pale text near the title block (a supersedes-type note) is not legible.

## What it tells the model

- TRAIN fw=75, fp=10 agrees with 75 teeth and 10 leaves.
- Drawn OD .8566 in = 21.7576 mm against the nominal m(z+2) = 21.56 mm (module .28): about 0.2 mm over, so tip allowance is generous; compare the model fourth wheel OD.
- Rim inside .700 in = 17.780 mm, hub .290 in = 7.366 mm, thickness .035 in = 0.889 mm.

## Hard to read

- .037 versus .048: both sit on the horizontal spoke; .037 is the arrow pair at the rim end, .048 near the hub (likely).
- Which of 75 or 72 belongs to which gap: 75 upper, 72 lower (likely).
- The .100: its arrows point to the pinion body step in the side view (likely).
