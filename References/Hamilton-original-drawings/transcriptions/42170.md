# 42170-2-01 Barrel Arbor, finished (transcription)

Source: References/Hamilton-original-drawings/42170-2-01.pdf (not in the repository). Legible at 300 dpi. Tolerances in units of the last decimal place.

## Title block
- BARREL ARBOR, FINISHED. Part no. 42170-2-01. Material "STEEL - S.A.E. 10120" (as written). Scale 5:1. Supersedes: "REDRAWN". Drawn K.R.A., checked B.L.H. Sheet letter M.
- Memos 10-22-43 and 4-29-44 (approved E.W.D.; no text). No original date.
- Notes: "POLISH" (three leaders: the left pivot, the .2295 bearings and the right ones), "START SPIRAL", ".005 AT 45", "1.00 R.".

## What it shows
The arbor side on, long axis horizontal: the square end at the left, the right pivot at the right; at left an end view of the collar with the square, the start-spiral ramp, and below an oblique detail of the hook slot.

## What it tells the model
- Body 10.160 mm dia (r 5.08) against the model's mainspring core r 5.4733 (4.9*1.117): the drawing is 7 % smaller. Collars 13.462 mm dia (the British Museum's arbor 13 mm). Collar span 18.288 mm for a strip 14.986 wide.
- Square 2.794 mm across flats, 3.683 over corners. Length 40.386 mm.
- Bearings 5.8293 mm (.2295) and pivots 4.3053 mm (.1695).

## Verification, 1 November 2026 (crops at 110-600 dpi, read with the scan open; images kept outside the repository)

Read on a 130 dpi crop of the whole sheet (scale from the 1.59: 654 px/in, every span below agrees to .01 in). **This corrects the first reading's turned profile, which was wrong (items 5 and 6 and the profile list above):**
- From the left end: the square and its domed end .410; the .1695 pivot .130 (to .540); the .2295 bearing from .540 to the boss at about .693 (from the drawn scale; not printed); then the body.
- The body is **.520 dia (the spring collar)**, .510 long, with a **.010 long boss of .400 dia at each end** (the two ".010" dimensions and the two ".400"s: the first reading took the .400 for the body between two .530 collars). The length over both bosses is **.530 +0/-.002**. The coils lie on the .520 body: a core radius of .260 in = 6.604 mm.
- **.720 +-.001** runs from the left boss's outer face to the right bearing's end (the shoulder to the right pivot); the right .2295 bearing is therefore .720 - .530 = .190 long; **.870 +-.002** runs from .540 to that shoulder; **1.000 +-.003** from .410 to that shoulder; the right pivot .180 (to 1.59).
- The hook slot **.120 +-.002** wide lies in the .520 body, and the **.325** runs from the left boss's outer face to the slot's far (right) edge, so the slot lies between .205 and .325. Its depth is not printed (about .07 on the scale; unsure).
- The end view (item 9): .520 dia; the start-spiral ramp 30 deg, its 1.00 R arc, .022, .150, .090, .210 and 45 deg remain unsure (which number is which of the ramp's lengths); the .110 +.004/-0 is across the square's flats, .145 +.005/-0 across corners.
- Tolerances by the last-place rule: 1.59 +-.01; 1.000 +-.003; .870 +-.002; .720 +-.001; .530 +0/-.002; .120 +-.002; .1695 +0/-.0003; .2295 +0/-.0003; .110 +.004/-0; .145 +.005/-0.
