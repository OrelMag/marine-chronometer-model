# 42001: Fusee chain assembly, transcribed

Source: `References/Hamilton-original-drawings/42001-2-01.pdf` (1 page; the PDF is never copied into the repository: NAWCC). Read at 300 dpi.

## Title block
- Title FUSEE CHAIN ASSEMBLY. Part no. 42001-2-01. Material blank. Scale 10:1. Supersedes REDRAWN. Drawn K.R.A.; checked B.L.H. (sure). Hamilton Watch Co., Lancaster, Pa.
- Memos (dates only, no text): MEMO. 11-2-42; MEMO. 6-3-43; MEMO. 10-20-43. Approval initials beside each memo look like E.W.D. (likely).
- Standing note (faint): ALL DIMENSIONS ARE GIVEN IN INCHES; TOLERANCES SHOWN ARE IN UNITS OF THE LAST DECIMAL PLACE.

## What the sheet shows
A plan of the chain at 10:1: the barrel-end hook (42002) at the left, the figure-eight links in a row broken by a wavy cut in the middle, the fusee-end hook (42003) at the right, centre lines through the pin holes; a side section below (the first joints hatched, the rest plain, with the break); a small end section at the right (three plates abreast with the rivet in the middle, the outer faces slightly convex). Only the first and last two joints are drawn.

## Parts list (verbatim)
1. PART NO. 42002 - 1 REQUIRED (barrel-end hook)
2. PART NO. 42003 - 1 REQUIRED (fusee-end hook)
3. PART NO. 42004 - 298 REQUIRED (link pin)
4. PART NO. 42005 - 148 REQUIRED (centre link)
5. PART NO. 42246 - 298 REQUIRED (outer link; its sheet is not in the set)

## Numbered items
1. Chain length: "297 SPACES @ .0940 = 27.92 ±.10". Pitch .0940 in = 2.3876 mm; length 27.92 in = 709.168 mm, ±.10 in (±2.54 mm), pin centre to pin centre from the first hook's hole to the last's. 297 x .0940 = 27.918, so 27.92 is rounded. Sure.
2. ".030 MAXIMUM" (0.7620 mm): arrow on the thickness of the fusee-end hook's shank in the side view. Sure for the number; likely that it limits the hooks' shank thickness (42002 draws it .0200).
3. Counts: 298 pins and 298 outer links but 148 centre links over 297 spaces. Three plates stand at each joint. The counts are as printed; I have not reconciled them (a strict alternation of two plates and one plate would give about 148 centre links; the outer links then number more than the spaces).

## Notes (verbatim)
- "ON THIS EDGE OF CHAIN ALL 3 LINKS AT EACH JOINT MUST BE COINCIDENT, AND SQUARE WITH FACE OF LINKS."
- "SLIGHT CONVEXITY ON OUTSIDE FACES OF LINKS DESIRABLE."
- "RIVET. FINISH FLUSH WITH FACES OF LINKS. #1 EMERY FINISH." (leader to a pin in the side section)
- "LINKS MUST FLEX FREELY ABOUT PINS WITH MINIMUM CLEARANCE AT ALL JOINTS TO INSURE MAXIMUM LATERAL RIGIDITY."
- "HARDEN AND DRAW AFTER ASSEMBLING."

## What it tells the model
- Drawn pitch 2.3876 mm (.0940 in), exact (297 x .0940 = 27.92 in = 709.2 mm). The model's chain (`movement.js`, `PC=2.44623`, the video's 2.19 mm times 1.117) is 2.5 % longer: the drawn pitch should replace it.
- 298 pins, 297 spaces, a hook at each end, three plates abreast at each joint, pins riveted flush with the plate faces, links with a slight convexity on the outer faces; the whole assembly hardened and drawn after riveting although each link, pin and hook sheet says "do not harden".

## Hard to read
- No number is unsure. The memo approval initials are a scrawl in a ruled cell (likely E.W.D.).

## Verification, 1 November 2026 (crops at 110-600 dpi, read with the scan open; images kept outside the repository)

- Item 3 (the counts): **settled, sure: they reconcile.** 297 spaces between 298 pins; each joint has three plates abreast. Every odd space (1, 3, ..., 297: 149 of them) takes a pair of outer links (42246), 298 plates in all; every even space (2, ..., 296: 148 of them) takes one centre link (42005), 148 plates. Each pin then carries a pair from one side and a single from the other (a pair in the first and last spaces, beside the hooks). That is 298 outer links and 148 centre links, as the parts list says.
- The memo 6-3-43 (between 11-2-42 and 10-20-43) is in the title block's memo list.
