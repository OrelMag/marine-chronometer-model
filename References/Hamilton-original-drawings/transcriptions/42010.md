## What it tells the model
- Drawn size: pivot to tip (row 4) 0.2280 in = 5.79 mm; boss R 1.27 mm; thickness 1.27 mm; hole 1.067 mm; countersink 2.032 mm. Overall box about 0.2257 in x 0.4 in (rows 4 to 6).
- Model (movement.js `WPO`, `WSH`, `WST`, pawl hole r 0.403 WFS with WFS = 16.55/14.53 = 1.139 after the scaling of the ratchet): its pawl hole radius 0.459 mm against the drawn 0.533 mm (.042 dia): about 14 % smaller than drawn. The drawn ratchet radii are 1.150 times the model's (see 42013, 42009), so a pawl scaled with the ratchet should agree to a few percent; the hole is the exception.
- 42027 (setup pawl) says "for other dimensions see 42010": the same outline.
