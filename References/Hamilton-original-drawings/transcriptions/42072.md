## What the sheet shows
A vertical side view of the arbor: top pivot, shoulder with a 60 deg cone, stem with an UNDERTURN, a collar, the ten-leaf pinion with THIRD WHEEL marking where that wheel meshes, a lower shoulder with a 60 deg cone, then the long lower pivot.

## What it tells the model

- TRAIN fp=10 agrees. Overall .940 in = 23.876 mm, pinion OD .1290 in = 3.277 mm, pinion length .260 in = 6.604 mm, pivots .0311 in = 0.790 mm; compare the model fourth arbor.

## Hard to read

- .0990: a slanting leader to the collar top edge; read .0990 (likely; .0999 not excluded).
- .060 +-2 at the lower shoulder: a diameter or a length (likely a length, shoulder to cone).
- Assignment of .445, .520 and .300 to their spans is likely, not certain.
