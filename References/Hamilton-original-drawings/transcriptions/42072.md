## Hard to read

- .0990: a slanting leader to the collar top edge; read .0990 (likely; .0999 not excluded).

## Verification, 1 November 2026 (crops at 110-600 dpi, read with the scan open; images kept outside the repository)

Read on a 250 dpi crop of the whole side view, with the lengths checked against the scale of the .940 (1245 px/in on the crop; every length below agrees with its dimension to 0.002 in).
- Item 7 (.0990): **settled, likely.** The leader ends on the chamfer at the collar's top edge; .0990 is the diameter of the flat on the collar's top face inside the chamfer (the collar itself is .1000 +-.0003).
- Item 11 (.060 +-2 at the lower shoulder): **settled, likely.** It is a diameter (arrows on both sides of the shoulder, as for .0565 and .0445): the lower shoulder is .060 +-.002 in diameter, with a 60 deg cone down to the .0311 pivot. Not a length.
- Items 18-22 (the lengths): **settled, sure.** Measured from the top tip: the top shoulder starts at .060 (item 16); .445 runs from there to the pinion's lower end (so the pinion ends .505 from the tip); .520 runs from the top shoulder to the lower shoulder's lower end (.580 from the tip); .300 runs from the collar's top face (.280 from the tip) to the lower shoulder's end; .260 runs from the pinion's top face (.320 from the tip) to the same end; .360 runs from there to the long pivot's tip (.580 + .360 = .940). Derived: the collar is .040 tall (.280 to .320), the leaves .185 long (.320 to .505), the lower stem .075 long (.505 to .580). **Correction: .260 is not the pinion's length**; the leaves are .185 (4.699 mm). Sure by arithmetic; the sheet's scale agrees to .003.
- Items 4-6, 10 (.0565, .0445, .0583 and the lower .0445): **still unsure** which stem diameter sits at which height. The sheet draws the stem about .058 wide from the shoulder to the collar; the .0445 leader at mid-stem and the .0583 leader above the collar are both arrows on the stem. Likely: .0565 just below the top cone, .0445 the undercut ("UNDERTURN"), .0583 the stem above the collar; the lower stem .0445 and the lower shoulder .060.
- Tolerances by the last-place rule: .0311 +0/-.0002; .0565 +-.0003; .0445 +-.0005; .0583 +-.0003; .1000 +-.0003; .1290 +.0005/-.0010; .060 +-.002; .0280 +0/-.0005; .445 +-.002; .520 +-.001; .260 +-.001; .300 +-.001.
