Source: `42093-2-01 (c.2).pdf` (the "(c.2)" is copy 2; the sheet number is 42093-2-01), 300 dpi.

## Title block
DETENT ASSEMBLY. Part no. 42093-2-01. Drawn ALLIED PROCESS ENGINEERS (printed in the block). Checked blank. Date 5-3-44; approval M.F.M. Material, scale and supersedes blank (no scale). Standing note on inches and tolerances. No dimensions anywhere on the sheet.

## What it shows
An exploded isometric at the left (parts pulled apart along their axes, leader lines to the labels) and the same assembled, smaller, at the right.

## Order shown in the exploded view (from the upper left of the sheet to the lower right, by position of the parts)
- Upper left: the detent adjusting screw (20756), a large flat-flanged slotted-head screw, its thread pointing down-right at the support block's end.
- Right of it: the detent support block (42086), a long block with two plain pins standing on its top, a tapped hole between them, an end hole for the adjusting screw and a slot across its front.
- Below the block, the detent (42087) lies beside it: a long bar with its foot at the left carrying a round hole, a slot and two steady pins, then the thin blade stepping down to a cross-piece and the jewel block at the end, with the trip-spring bracket's leg alongside.
- Left of the detent's foot, the washer (42251) and below it the big slotted detent clamp screw (37204), pointing up-right at the foot's hole.
- Right of the block, two small slotted-head screws pointing down-left into the block's front slot: the clamp screw and the lock adjusting screw (both 42091).
- Lower middle, under the detent's jewel-block end: the trip spring support bracket (42092), a bent Z-shaped strip with two holes, the trip spring (42088), a long tapered strip below it, and the two small 1770 screws at the lower left, each aimed at a bracket or spring hole.
The stacking along each axis is by picture position (likely); the order printed is only the parts list's.

## Numbered items
No dimensions, no notes. Counts: one of each of the 11 lines.

## What it tells the model
- Eleven catalogue lines make the detent group. The model tags 42086, 42092, 20756 and the 1770 screws in `movement.js` (around lines 1219 to 1250); I did not check that 42091 (both screws), 42251 and 37204 are tagged. Both small screws are 1770, the same screw. The washer lies between the detent clamp screw's head and the detent foot.

## Hard to read
- The last digit of "42087" on its label is crossed by the leader line (sure all the same). Nothing else is unsure; the stacking order is from the picture, not printed.
