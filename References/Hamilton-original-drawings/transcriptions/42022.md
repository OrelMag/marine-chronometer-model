## What it tells the model

- Overall 2.000 in = 50.800 mm; bearings .1695 in = 4.305 mm; square .110 in = 2.794 mm; knurled body .2500 in = 6.350 mm.
- No wind-indicator pinion teeth are drawn; part 42018 pressed in at the left end is presumably it.

## Verification, 1 November 2026 (crops at 110-600 dpi, read with the scan open; images kept outside the repository)

Read on a 130 dpi crop of the whole side view, scale from the .375 (539 px/in); each span below agrees with its number to .01 in.
- **The lengths are settled, sure** (the earlier "likely" ones): .375 from the left end to the body step; the pressed part 42018 fills the first .115 (.375 - .260); .260 the left bearing (.1695 dia) from the part's end to the body step; .360 from the body step to the collar's right face (the body's left part, .2295); .310 from the cross hole's centre to the collar's right face; .045 the collar (.316 dia); .060 the plain gap after the collar; .080 the knurl's width (the knurl, .2500 dia, is only .080 wide, next to the gap); then the body to .875 from the body step; .750 the shank, of which .410 is the .1695 shank and .340 the square.
- Item 18: **settled, sure.** The .110 +.004/-0 is across flats of the square on the end view; the .145 +.005/-0 dia is the round the square is cut from. Item 20: the pressed part's bore .0490 and the arbor's recess .0500 +-.0002 (press fit); likely.
- Tolerances by the last-place rule: .375 +-.005; .875 +-.001; .750 +-.005; .260 +-.002; .360 +-.001; .310 +-.001; .045 +-.001; .410 +-.005; .1695 +0/-.0003; .2295 +0/-.0003; .316 +-.001; .2500 +-.0002; .110 +.004/-0; .145 +.005/-0; .0500 +-.0002; .036 +0/-.001.
