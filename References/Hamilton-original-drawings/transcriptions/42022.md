## What it tells the model

- Overall 2.000 in = 50.800 mm; bearings .1695 in = 4.305 mm; square .110 in = 2.794 mm; knurled body .2500 in = 6.350 mm.
- No wind-indicator pinion teeth are drawn; part 42018 pressed in at the left end is presumably it.

## Hard to read

- The short chain of lengths .260 / .360 / .310 above the left half: assigned to spans by the arrows, likely.
- .110 is on the end view of the square, .145 the round shank (likely).
- .060 and .080 near the knurl: no tolerances, spans likely.
