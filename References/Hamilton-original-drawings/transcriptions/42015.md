- Items 4, 5: **settled, sure.** 1.400 is the recess diameter (the wall at the rim land's inside); 1.460 is where the tooth ring starts (the root/step diameter). The rim land is .030 wide radially.
- **Left face**: a stepped recess of three diameters: .600 about .015 deep (read off the scale, +-.004, likely), .470 to about .055 deep, .320 to .060 deep; the .005 at item 18 is the step between the .470 and .320 floors (sure); the web behind is .040 (item 16, the .600/.320 floor to the central disc's face). Items 7, 8, 9 (.600, .470, .320) are the diameters of those three steps: **settled, likely** (not the pair of hub steps of the first reading).
- Item 19/20 (the .053 hole at .6100): unchanged, sure; r .610 lies in the web inside the 1.400 recess, outside the .740 disc.
