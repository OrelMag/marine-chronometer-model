## Verification, 1 November 2026 (crops at 110-600 dpi, read with the scan open; images kept outside the repository)

Read on a 160 dpi crop.
- Item 2 (65 deg) and item 9 (.030): **settled, likely** (with 42013, which shows it better). The 65 deg arc sits between the near-vertical tooth line through the tip and the sloping tooth line to its left: the included angle between a tooth's locking face and its ramp. The .030 is the offset between the centre line and the locking face's line (the line leans, so the faces are not radial: tangent to a circle of .030 about the centre).
- Item 8 (.235) and item 7 (.220 R finish lines): **still unsure.** The .235 dimension runs from the square's lower corner to the left end of the finish-line arc's leader; its length on the sheet is not to scale (about .30 in). It belongs to the ray finish's lines, which are surface marks, so it does not change the part's shape.
- Items 5, 6 (.115 +.001/-0 across flats, .155 minimum across corners): sure; the square is drawn turned 45 deg (a diamond).
- Tolerances: .670 +-.002; .115 +.001/-0; .048 +0/-.002.
