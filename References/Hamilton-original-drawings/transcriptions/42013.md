## What it tells the model
- Drawn: 40 teeth, OD 17.018 mm (r 8.509), root 15.773 mm, thickness 1.905 mm, bore 5.842 mm, holes 11.176 mm apart.
- Model (movement.js `WRT={z:40,m:7.4/20.95,d:1.5}`, tips r 7.4 mm since 20 October 2026): drawn r 8.509 is 1.150 times the tips r 7.4 as written in the code (the FK scaling in the file multiplies the module; whether the x1.117 rescale of 1 November already changed this should be checked on the built model). The same ratio 1.151 appears for the sustaining ratchet (42009: r 19.05 against 16.55). Tooth depth: model d = 1.5 module = 0.53 mm against 0.622 drawn (1.17 times). Model tooth form 'radial' (steep face along a radius), the drawing's .030 offset puts the locking face about 5.5 deg off radial at the root radius (asin .030/.3105).
- The two countersunk holes (winding ratchet screws) are not checked against the model here.

## Verification, 1 November 2026 (crops at 110-600 dpi, read with the scan open; images kept outside the repository)

Read on a 110 dpi crop.
- Item 4 (65 deg) and item 5 (.030): **settled, likely.** The 65 deg arc lies between the tooth tip's near-vertical line and the sloping line of a tooth's ramp: the included angle between the locking face and the ramp. The .030 is the offset of that near-vertical line (the locking face, extended down to the centre height) from the centre line: the faces lean, tangent to a .030 circle, about 5.5 deg off radial at the root.
- Item 11 (.060): **settled, likely.** It is the depth from the face to the bottom of the 45 deg countersink, with the .077 land .015 deep below it.
- Tolerances: .670 +-.002; .230 +.001/-0; .146 +-.002; .077 +-.001; .075 +0/-.002.
