# 6MX / DEC staging: Russian-language web and dg-chrono.de (14 Oct 2026)

> Staging catalogue as the search wrote it (14 October 2026). Only a curated part of these files is committed, in `photos/` and `documents/` beside this file; the rest stays in E:\mc-video\wempe-staging. Local names here are the staging names; the committed copies keep the same file names.

Images and PDFs only (no videos). Not for the repository: other people's photographs, cited, never committed.
dg-chrono.de photographs are all by Johannes Altmeppen (watermark "(c) Johannes Altmeppen 2017"); the site is the Deutsche Gesellschaft fuer Chronometrie's "Russische Uhren" chapter, which draws on Altmeppen/Dittrich, "Das Deutsche Einheits-Chronometer" (Heel).

dg-chrono series naming (the author's own, consistent across every chronometer): Z = dial; A = Aufsicht, top-plate side square-on, movement in a 4-arm holder on mm graph paper; uZ = unter Zifferblatt, pillar-plate dial side square-on; N/O/S/W = the movement out of its case seen obliquely (about 25-35 deg up) from four sides; Feder/F = the detent between the plates; Logo = signature; Kasten = box. Images are 1024 px on the long side (the site's largest; no larger originals are linked).

Usefulness: high = settles geometry (square-on with scale, or a clear side profile); medium = shape/finish/assembly but oblique or small; low = documents, marks, context.
