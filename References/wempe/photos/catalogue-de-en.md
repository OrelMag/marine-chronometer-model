# Deutsches Einheits-Chronometer: German- and English-web sources (staging)

> Staging catalogue as the search wrote it (14 October 2026). Only a curated part of these files is committed, in `photos/` and `documents/` beside this file; the rest stays in E:\mc-video\wempe-staging. Local names here are the staging names; the committed copies keep the same file names.

Collected 14 October 2026 for the 3D model of the Einheitschronometer (Wempe Hamburg / A. Lange & Söhne Glashütte; post-war Lange VEB / GUB; Soviet 6MX copy).
Images and PDFs only; videos are listed with timestamps and are not downloaded.
Usefulness for measurement: **high** = square-on or clean side view of the movement at good resolution; **medium** = the movement or case recognisable but oblique, small or partly hidden; **low** = context only.

Readers should note the variants: (W) = Wempe wartime; (L) = Lange wartime Einheits (Nos. 5001-5870, 1943-47); (V) = Lange VEB / GUB post-war (Glashütte numbering restarts; "Kal. 100"-style name used by some auctioneers); (P) = post-war Wempe; (S) = Soviet 6MX (1MChZ Kirova / Poljot).

## Text sources (no images downloaded)

- watch-wiki "Einheitschronometer" (https://watch-wiki.org/index.php?title=Einheitschronometer) and Knirim / Christian Schmiedchen "Marine-Chronometer aus Glashütte" (http://www.knirim.de/schmied.htm): the list of Glashütte Einheits features against Lange's earlier Kal. 100 (quoted in the report).
- Knirim "Wempe Chronometerwerke Hamburg" (http://www.knirim.de/wempe.htm): Wempe numbering from 2800 (1942), three pillars, war production and dispersal.
- dg-chrono.de "Wempe Einheitschronometer" (link above): Altmeppen's drawing collection by group (05 01 Gehäuse ... 05 08 Gestell; 51 00 Holzgehäuse), with drawing dates.
- uhrforum.de "Lange Einheitschronometer" (https://uhrforum.de/threads/lange-einheitschronometer.48468/) post #10 by JohannesB: genuine Lange/Wempe against Soviet copies.
- uhrforum.de "Wempe Marinechronometer" (https://uhrforum.de/threads/wempe-marinechronometer.48668/): post-war Wempe No. 6774 (1959), chain and fusee; its photos (abload.de) are gone.
- Auction texts: Uhren-Muser 95th lot 86 (Lange 5373) and 96th lot 250 (Lange 5499); Dorotheum Lange 5024; Fellows 26 Mar 2013 lot 893 (Wempe 8725, "three-pillar chain-fusee movement"); Christie's lot 941490 (Wempe 4824, c.1950, "black composition bowl with steel gimbal", balance signed RGG); Antiquorum (Wempe 6161, 8711).
- Book: Altmeppen & Dittrich, *Das Deutsche Einheits-Chronometer*, HEEL Verlag 2012, ISBN 978-3-86852-597-7, 192 or 208 pp., €69.90; no preview or table of contents online.
