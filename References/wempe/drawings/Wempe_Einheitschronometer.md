# Wempe Einheits-Chronometer — „Federchronometer 05“ Cross-Section Callout Map

## 1. Document Provenance & Numbering System
* **Drawing Title:** `Federchronometer 05`
* **Credit / Watermark:** `© Johannes Altmeppen (by courtesy of Gerhard D. Wempe KG) 2017`
* **Original Artifact:** Hand-colored cross-section hanging in the Wempe Observatory (*Sternwarte*) in Glashütte; reproduced on the front and back inside endpapers (*Deckel-Innenseiten*) of Johannes Altmeppen & Prof. Dr. Herbert Dittrich, *„Das Deutsche Einheits-Chronometer – Chronometer für Marine und Luftwaffe von Wempe, A. Lange & Söhne, Poljot“* (Heel Verlag, 2011/2012).
* **Part Numbering Format (`05 XX XX - X`):**
  * `05`: Model prefix for the *Federchronometer* (spring-detent marine chronometer; `04` = *Ankerchronometer*).
  * Second digit pair (`01`–`08`): Sub-assembly (*Baugruppe*):
    * `05 01` — *Gehäuse* (Brass case, bezel, gimbal & winding key)
    * `05 02` — *Zeigerwerk / Auf- u. Abwerk* (Motion work, hands, power-reserve indicator & dial)
    * `05 03` — *Unruh & Spirale* (Balance wheel & helical hairspring assembly)
    * `05 04` — *Hemmung* (Spring detent escapement & balance staff)
    * `05 05` — *Laufwerk* (Going train wheels, pinions & maintaining click)
    * `05 06` — *Schnecke* (Fusee cone, great wheel, maintaining power & stop-work)
    * `05 07` — *Federhaus* (Mainspring barrel, arbor, ratchet & fusee chain)
    * `05 08` (`05 09`) — *Gestell* (Plates, pillars, bridges, cocks, bushings & jewel settings)
  * `WN XXX`: *Wempe-Norm* factory standard hardware (`Schraube` = screw, `Stift` = pin).
