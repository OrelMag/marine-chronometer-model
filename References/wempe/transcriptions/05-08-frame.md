# Wempe works drawings, group 05-08 (Gestell: the frame: plates, cocks, bridges, pillars, movement ring), transcribed

Source folder: `References/wempe/drawings/05-08/`, 21 JPEG scans (the brief said 22; the folder holds 21: 01a, 01b, 02, 03a, 03b, 04 to 14, 30, 31, 32-1, 32-2, 33). Sheets 30, 31, 32-1 and 33 are for the **lever (Anker) chronometer** (drawing numbers 127-556.04 ...); 32-2 is the detent chronometer's own Unterplatte kompl. (05 08 02), drawn to the same plan as the lever's 32-1. Every table cell was read on crops enlarged 3× to 10×. In the tables, `"` is the sheet's ditto mark (repeat the letter, or the value, above it); a value I could not read for certain carries "?" or both alternatives. All sizes in mm; German decimal commas kept as printed.

## How the hole coordinates are defined (read across the sheets)

- **Origin:** hole 1, "Minutenrad" (the centre wheel's arbor, which carries the minute hand), N 0,0 O 0,0, on both plates. The plate is an 85 mm disc (Fertigmaß 85ϕ h6) centred on it: on 03b the rim lies 412 px from hole 1 at 9,74 px/mm = 42,3 mm (scaled, not dimensioned; it agrees with 42,5). So the origin is the plate's centre (inferred from the drawing, not stated in words).
- **Axes:** N/S along the vertical centre line, O/W (Ost/West, east/west) along the horizontal one. The sheets do not say in words which way N points on the dial; it is inferred: hole 2 "Auf u. Ab" (the up-and-down indicator) is at N 23,0 and hole 35 "Sekundenrad" (the seconds wheel) at S 23,0, so **N is towards XII and S towards VI** (inferred: the usual Wempe/Einheits-Chronometer dial has the up-and-down at XII and small seconds at VI). In every plan in this group (01b, 03b, 30, 31, 32-1, 32-2) N is up and O to the right: hole 10 (fusee, O 16,708) is right of centre, hole 45 (barrel, W 24,784) left. Both plates are drawn with the same handedness, so the coordinates are one frame through the whole movement (the same arbor has the same numbers on both plates). From which face the plans are seen (dial side or back) is not stated; whether O is the dial's III or IX is therefore **not settled by these sheets** (open).
- **Numbers:** the same hole number means the same place on every sheet (plates, cocks, bridges), e.g. 23 Unruh S 12,110 O 9,10 on 01a, 03a, 04, 05, 30.
- **Table columns:** Nr. | Benennung | Koordinaten (N/S) | Koordinaten (O/W) | Körner od. stanzen (centre-punch, or punch to this diameter) | bohren (drill) | kalibr. (ream / gauge size; or the thread, e.g. "M 2,6", when the hole is tapped).
