## Federchronometer 05 (coloured full section, no drawing number) — `Wempe_Einheitschronometer.jpg`

- **Title block:** none. Title lettered at the top right, "Federchronometer 05" (sure). Copyright line bottom right: "© Johannes Altmeppen (by courtesy of Gerhard D. Wempe KG) 2017" (a modern photograph's caption, not part of the drawing). No scale, no date, no material, no dimensions on the sheet.
- **What it shows:** one coloured, hatched sectional elevation of the whole movement in its ring (2048 × 828 px). Brass is coloured yellow (hatched where cut), steel blue, jewels red, the mainspring and hairspring dark blue/violet, the hands black. The section is an unrolled section through the arbors (not one straight diameter), so horizontal distances between arbors are NOT plan distances. Orientation: the **top plate (Oberplatte) is at the top**, the **pillar plate (Unterplatte) below it and the dial (Zifferblatt) at the bottom**; the winding square of the fusee points up (the case back). From left to right the section runs through: the mounting ring (Werkring) and a steady pin at the far left; the **barrel** (Federhaus, with its bridge above the top plate level, set-up ratchet and click); the up-and-down (Auf und Ab) work under the pillar plate; the **fusee** with its maintaining work and, above it, the winding hole's dust bush in a raised housing; the centre (minute) arbor with the motion work and the hour and minute hands; the third (Zwischenrad) arbor; the fourth (seconds, Sekundenrad) arbor with the seconds hand; the detent (Gangfeder) with its stones; the escape wheel (Gangrad); the **balance** (Unruh) between the upper cock (Unruhkloben oben) and the lower cock (Unruhkloben unten), with the hairspring (Spiralfeder) above; then a **pillar** (Pfeiler) with its two screws, and the Werkring with the dial at the far right.
- **Dimensions:** none on the sheet. See "Heights" below for pixel measurements (scaled, not dimensioned).
