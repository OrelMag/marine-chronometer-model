# Wempe works drawings, group 05-03 + 05-04: balance and hairspring (Unruh), escapement (Hemmung) — transcription

Files: every .jpg in `References/wempe/drawings/05-03/` (10) and `05-04/` (14), 24 in all. Crops for every reading were enlarged 3–16× (scratchpad, not in the repository). Decimal commas are kept as on the sheets ("4,5" = 4.5 mm).

**Which chronometer each sheet belongs to (inferred from the numbers, not written on the sheets):** the sheets carry three numbering systems. "05 xx yy" is the Einheits-Chronometer's (spring detent, fusee). "127-556.04 …" with a second number "04 …" belongs to the **Anker-Chronometer** (lever escapement): 05-04_20 "Hemmung für Ankerchronometer" and 05-04_21 "Anker kompl." carry it, and so do 05-03_1 (Unruh kompl., whose plan shows a lever's roller with a "Hebelstein"), 05-03_5 (Spiralfeder kompl., part 34 of 05-03_1's parts list) and 05-03_7 (Spiralkurve). Second numbers "02 xx yy" go with "Kaliber 020…" on 05-04_01 (the spring-detent escapement): 05-04_06 Heberolle 020402, 05-04_23 Gangrad Zahnform 02 04 08 a, 05-03_6 Spiralfaden 020310. 05-04_05 (040401-1, Unruhwelle) has a 04-series number, re-numbered in pencil 05 04 02-1. 05-03_3-1 (17 04 01, a 5,73 mm staff) matches neither and is likely another instrument's. Sheets 05-04_20, 05-04_21 (and the balance 05-03_1) are **lever-escapement (Anker-Chronometer) sheets** and are marked so below.
