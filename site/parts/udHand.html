Notes. The sheet's collar is drawn up from the 0.6 strip; the page models it as a Ø 3.0 pipe, and so does this recipe (estimated, README).

Where the page's model departs from this recipe (the drawing's figure is this sheet's): the page's blade is a separate closed piece whose root is an 8-segment arc on the boss's radius, against the boss's 72-sided wall: a hairline gap (0.003) the check reads as a face with metal both sides of it, not a hole the page has and the recipe hasn't; the recipe joins them in one solid
