Notes. Every size is an estimate from photographs of Kirov No. 247 and Romanov's figures; no works drawing of the 6MX's contact exists. The numbers are the page's (dims.js CONTACT6, CT6). The page draws the foot and each tine as separate pieces (the tines' lower ends overlap the foot's top by 0.016); the screws that hold the foot to the bowl go through the wall into it, and the page draws no holes for them in the foot (a maker drills and taps them). The foot's black insulating strip and the screws, washers and terminals are bought lines.

Threads drawn by convention (DIN ISO 6410): the STEP solid has an outside thread at its major diameter, an inside one at its minor (tap drill).
