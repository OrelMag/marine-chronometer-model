Notes. The groove is built as ruled surfaces over splines through the 05 06_20 curve (about a micron from it); a CNC lathe cuts it as a screw thread of changing core radius, the floor radius at each quarter turn as listed.

Where the page's model departs from this recipe (the drawing's figure is this sheet's): the page sweeps the flange as two closed pieces and the band round the whole turn, meeting on the seam at 0°: their end caps are faces inside the metal, which the check cannot always tell from a surface the page's 216 rays round the slot's cutter arc, whose floor climbs 0.4 mm between two rays (1.7°), chord it up to 0.1 off the circle at the groove's end N the page's rays (216 and the two at N) join the last land's floor to the hub across a chord up to 0.13 off the wall
