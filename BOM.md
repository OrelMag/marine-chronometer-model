## Known deviations

- in 42055.tb#2 -> 42059 (tap): The manual has all three upper train bridge screws in pillars (Ops. 14, 47, 48). The model's third screw is where the top-view photographs show one, at (-8.5, 27.7); a pillar there would stand in the fourth wheel, whose size the layout fixes, so it is drawn threaded into the bridge alone (IDEAS.md 1.2). What it holds in the real movement is not settled.
