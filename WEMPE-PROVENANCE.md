# The Wempe's parts against their drawings and photographs

Started 25 October 2026: every part of the Wempe Einheits-Chronometer model, as last revised, compared with its works drawing (`References/wempe/drawings/`, read off in `References/wempe/transcriptions/`) and checked against photographs of real ones (`References/wempe/photos/`, chiefly Wempe 3955; `tools/photocompare/`). It follows the sheet-by-sheet pass of 15 October 2026 (RESOLVED.md, the Wempe's entries) and the photographs of 18 October (README, "Wempe 3955 as photographed"), after the changes since (the physics pass, the builds, the Steg, the jewels, the transit lock).

The drawings decide the geometry; a photograph shows one clock, so where it differs from a sheet the difference is recorded as that clock's, not the model's error. Parts are taken in order of their effect on the timekeeping: the escapement, balance and hairspring; the fusee, mainspring, chain and maintaining work; the train; the frame; the case, dial and hands. Fixes go in in batches of five or six, each batch checked and released; each fix also has its entry in RESOLVED.md.

Columns: the part (its `hn`), its sheet, the photographs it was checked on, the verdict (matches, differs, a feature missing, unverifiable), how sure, and what was done or is left.
